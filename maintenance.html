Keep Your Car Looking Its Best Between Full Details

Once your car has been fully detailed, the key to keeping it in that condition is regular maintenance. A maintenance detail is a lighter, more focused service designed to refresh your car and address any build up that has occurred since your last visit. It is far more efficient than starting from scratch each time and keeps your car at a consistently high standard throughout the year. Think of it as an ongoing investment in your car's appearance and protection rather than a one off clean.

Step by Step

What's Included

A thorough but efficient service to keep your car at the standard set by your original full detail.

Step 01

Pre Rinse & Snow Foam

The vehicle is pre rinsed and a snow foam is applied to dwell and loosen any surface dirt and grime before any contact washing begins.

Step 02

Wheels & Arches

Wheels and wheel arches are cleaned with dedicated products to remove brake dust and grime, keeping them looking as sharp as the rest of the car.

Step 03

Safe Hand Wash

The full exterior is hand washed using the two bucket method with soft microfibre mitts to safely remove surface contamination without introducing any new marks.

Step 04

Rinse & Dry

The vehicle is thoroughly rinsed and carefully dried with clean microfibre drying towels to leave a streak free, spot free finish across all panels.

Step 05

Sealant Top Up

A spray sealant is applied to all painted surfaces to refresh the protection layer and maintain the hydrophobic properties from your original full detail.

Step 06

Interior Refresh

A quick interior vacuum and wipe down of all surfaces, dashboard, door cards and glass to keep the inside fresh and tidy between deeper cleans.

Why It Matters

Why Regular Maintenance Makes Sense

Letting a car go between details means more contamination, more potential for paint damage and a bigger job every time. Regular maintenance visits keep your car at a consistently high level, protect your investment and cost far less over time than repeatedly deep cleaning a neglected car. Once you have had a full detail, maintenance is the smart way to keep it that way.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is this only available to full detail customers?
A maintenance detail is designed to maintain a car that is already in great condition. If a car has not been fully detailed first, a maintenance visit will not achieve the same results and will not address the deeper cleaning and correction the car needs. Starting with a full detail sets the right foundation.
How often should I book a maintenance detail?
Most customers book every 4 to 8 weeks depending on how much the car is used and how quickly it picks up dirt. We are happy to advise you on the right frequency for your car and how you use it when you get in touch.
How long does a maintenance detail take?
Most maintenance visits take between 1 and 1.5 hours depending on vehicle size and condition. Because the car is already well maintained the process is significantly quicker than a full detail.
I haven't had a full detail yet. What should I book?
Start with our Full Detail, which covers a complete exterior and interior deep clean to bring your car up to the standard it needs to be at. Once that is done you can keep it there with regular maintenance visits.
Do you cover my area?
We cover Glasgow and the surrounding areas. Not sure if we reach you? Drop us a message on WhatsApp and we will confirm within minutes.
