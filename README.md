# Mearns Detailers Website

A responsive business website designed and developed for **Mearns Detailers**, a mobile car detailing business operating across Glasgow.

This project was built for a real operating business, with a focus on creating a professional online presence, clearly presenting services and converting visitors into customer enquiries.

## Live Website

[View Live Website](https://mearnsdetailers.com)

## Overview

I designed and developed the website from the ground up to support the day-to-day operations and marketing of Mearns Detailers.

The website provides customers with information about detailing services, pricing and the business, while providing clear routes for potential customers to request a quote.

## Features

- Responsive design for desktop and mobile devices
- Dedicated pages for individual detailing services
- Customer quote and enquiry flow
- Before and after project imagery
- Service and pricing information
- Blog section with multiple articles
- Search engine optimisation through structured content, sitemap and robots.txt
- Mobile-friendly navigation
- Clear call-to-action elements throughout the website
- Production deployment through Netlify

## What I Learned

Building this project gave me practical experience developing and maintaining a website used by a real business.

I gained experience in responsive web development, structuring a multi-page website, improving user journeys, managing production assets, implementing basic SEO practices and deploying a live website.

Working on a website tied directly to my own business also helped me understand how technical decisions can affect customer experience, lead generation and wider business objectives.

## About Mearns Detailers

Mearns Detailers is a mobile car detailing business serving Glasgow and surrounding areas. I founded the business and developed this website as its primary online presence.
