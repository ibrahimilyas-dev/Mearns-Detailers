A Clean Engine Bay Makes a Real Difference

Most people never think about the engine bay until something goes wrong. A build up of grease, dirt and grime makes it harder to spot leaks, corrosion or worn components. A professionally cleaned engine bay not only looks impressive when you open the bonnet, it also makes routine maintenance easier and helps protect components from long term deterioration. Whether you are preparing for a sale, a show or just want to keep your car in top condition throughout, an engine bay detail is one of the most overlooked but most satisfying services we offer.

Step by Step

What's Included

Every engine bay detail follows a careful process to clean thoroughly without causing any damage to sensitive components.

Step 01

Component Protection

Sensitive electrical components, fuse boxes and air intakes are carefully covered and protected before any cleaning begins to ensure no water or product causes damage.

Step 02

Degreaser Application

A professional engine degreaser is applied across the entire bay, breaking down built up grease, oil residue and road grime that has accumulated over time.

Step 03

Agitation & Detail Brushing

Detail brushes are used to agitate and loosen stubborn build up around hoses, wiring looms, brackets and tight spaces that a cloth or sponge cannot reach.

Step 04

Low Pressure Rinse

The bay is carefully rinsed using low pressure water to remove all degreaser and loosened contamination without forcing water into sensitive areas.

Step 05

Dry Down

The engine bay is dried using microfibre towels and air blasting to remove standing water from crevices, ensuring everything is dry before dressing is applied.

Step 06

Trim & Plastic Dressing

A trim dressing is applied to all plastics, rubber hoses and covers to restore a clean, factory fresh appearance and provide protection against drying and cracking.

Why It Matters

The Benefits of an Engine Bay Detail

A clean engine bay is not just about looks. Removing built up grease and dirt helps prevent corrosion, makes it easier to spot leaks or worn parts during routine checks, and can even help your car run slightly cooler by improving airflow. It also adds real value if you are looking to sell, as a clean engine bay signals a well maintained vehicle to any potential buyer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to clean an engine bay with water?
Yes, when done correctly. We protect all sensitive electrical components before any water or product is applied, and use low pressure rinsing throughout. Modern engines are designed to handle moisture and our process is safe when carried out properly.
How long does an engine bay detail take?
Most engine bay details take between 25-40 minutes depending on the size of the bay and how much build up there is. Very heavily soiled bays may take a little longer to do properly.
Can I book this alongside another service?
Absolutely. The engine bay detail is one of our most popular add-ons alongside a full detail, exterior detail or machine polish. Just select it as an add-on when booking or mention it when you message us.
Will the dressing make my engine bay look greasy?
No. We use a quality trim dressing that leaves a clean, satin finish rather than a greasy or overly shiny look. The result is a natural, well maintained appearance rather than anything overdone.
