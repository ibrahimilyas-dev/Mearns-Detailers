Step By Step

What's Included In Your Full Detail

Every full detail follows the same thorough process from start to finish, inside and out.

Step 01

Pre Wash

We apply a pre wash to loosen surface dirt, road grime and light soiling before any physical contact is made with the paintwork, minimising the risk of scratching.

Step 02

Wheels & Tyres

Wheels are cleaned with dedicated wheel cleaner and brushes to remove brake dust and grime. Tyre sidewalls are scrubbed clean and dressed for a rich, finished look.

Step 03

Two Bucket Safe Wash

Using the two bucket method with soft mitts, we hand wash the entire vehicle, then dry carefully with microfibre towels to prevent water spots.

Step 04

Chemical Decontamination

Iron fallout remover and tar remover are applied to dissolve embedded contamination that a wash alone cannot remove, preparing the surface for protection.

Step 05

4 Month Wax Sealant

A professional grade wax sealant is applied to all painted surfaces and glass, providing hydrophobic protection that lasts up to four months.

Step 06

Full Interior Vacuum

Every area of the interior is vacuumed including seats, carpets, boot and under the seats, removing all loose dirt and debris before the deep clean begins.

Step 07

Steam Clean

High temperature steam sanitises and deep cleans the dashboard, console, door cards, vents and buttons, killing bacteria and lifting ingrained grime.

Step 08

Plastics & Trim Dressed

All interior plastics, door cards and trim panels are wiped down and dressed to restore a clean, natural finish and protect against fading and cracking.

Step 09

All Windows Cleaned

Interior and exterior glass is cleaned to remove the greasy film that builds up over time, leaving every window streak free and crystal clear.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a full detail take?
Most full details take between 1.5 and 2.5 hours depending on vehicle size and condition. We will give you an estimated time when you book.
Do I need to provide water or power?
Yes, we do require access to a power source and water supply to carry out the service.
Will this remove scratches and swirl marks?
The full detail uses a safe wash method to avoid introducing new swirls but does not include paint correction. For scratch and swirl removal you would need our Machine Polishing or Ceramic Coating packages.
Do you cover my area?
We cover Glasgow and the surrounding areas. Not sure if we reach you? Drop us a message on WhatsApp and we will confirm within minutes.
